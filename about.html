About Us

Our cookie cutter journey began through our first business, Bailey and Friends, where we create homemade organic dog treats. As we spent more time baking for pups and connecting with other pet treat businesses, we realized how hard it could be to find fun, unique cutters that truly fit the personality of a brand—or that customers would love buying for their pets. That inspired us to start designing our own. Today, we create playful, creative cookie cutters with pet lovers and fellow small business owners in mind. Whether you’re baking treats for your own dog or for your customers, our goal is to help make every treat a little more special, memorable, and fun.
